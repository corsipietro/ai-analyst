### 1. The Debris Threshold
The growth of debris $N$ is governed by the balance between new debris created by collisions and the natural decay of existing debris:
$$\Delta N \approx f \cdot c - \frac{N}{\tau}$$

$$\Delta N \approx (f \cdot \alpha \cdot S \cdot N) - \frac{N}{\tau}$$
Debris grows when $(f \cdot \alpha \cdot S) > \frac{1}{\tau}$. We can calculate the critical number of satellites ($S_{crit}$) required to maintain the debris level:
$$S_{crit} = \frac{1}{f \cdot \alpha \cdot \tau} = \frac{1}{100 \cdot 10^{-6} \cdot 25} = 400 \text{ satellites [computed]}$$
