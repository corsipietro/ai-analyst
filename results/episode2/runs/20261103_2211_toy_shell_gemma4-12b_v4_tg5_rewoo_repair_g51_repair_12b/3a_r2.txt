*   **Model vs. Reality:** These results are from a simplified model where $\alpha$ and $f$ were chosen to make behavior visible, not to reflect real-world orbital mechanics.
*   **Missing Variables:** The data does not explicitly show the collision count $c$ or the satellite population $S$ over time, which would allow for a more precise calculation of the "break-even" point where $f \cdot c = N/\tau$.
*   **Future Work:** To confirm the exact threshold, a sensitivity analysis on the launch rate $L$ between 80 and 100 would be needed to identify the specific tipping point where growth becomes sustained.
