### Limits and Scope
These results are based on the specific parameters of the toy model (e.g., $\alpha = 10^{-6}$). In a real system, the collision coefficient $\alpha$ and the orbital dynamics are much more complex. These results only represent the "greedy" strategy within the 60-year window provided by the simulation. To make these conclusions firmer, a longer time horizon would be needed to see if the 120-launch case eventually reaches a catastrophic "Kessler Syndrome" state where $S$ collapses.
