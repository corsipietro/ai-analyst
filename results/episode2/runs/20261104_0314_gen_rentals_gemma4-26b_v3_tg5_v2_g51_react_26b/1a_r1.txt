### Data Quality and Reliability Issues
The following issues "are not solid" and should be considered before making business decisions:

**1. Missing Information (Incompleteness)**
*   **Customer Profiles**: 23 customer records are missing age (`eta`) data [tool].
*   **Trip Details**: 155 rentals are missing the duration (`durata_min`) [tool], and 140 rentals are missing the arrival station (`stazione_arrivo`) [tool]. This means you cannot accurately calculate average trip lengths or popular destination trends for a portion of the data.

**2. Relational and Integrity Issues**
*   **Orphaned Records**: There are **11 rentals** associated with `cliente_id` values that do not exist in the customer database [tool]. This prevents these specific trips from being attributed to a specific customer profile (e.g., you cannot know if a "premium" or "base" user made those trips).

**3. Statistical Caution**
*   **User Bias**: Because a single customer can have many rentals (up to 317 recorded in this dataset), the average revenue or distance per *rental* is not the same as the average per *customer* [notes]. Any analysis that treats every rental as an independent observation of a "typical user" will be biased toward your most frequent riders [inference].

**Conclusion**
The data is sufficient for a high-level overview of total revenue and total distance. However, any analysis regarding **customer behavior** (due to orphans and missing ages) or **station popularity/flow** (due to missing arrival stations) will be incomplete. I recommend cleaning the station duplicates and investigating the cause of the 11 orphaned rental records before performing deeper profiling.
