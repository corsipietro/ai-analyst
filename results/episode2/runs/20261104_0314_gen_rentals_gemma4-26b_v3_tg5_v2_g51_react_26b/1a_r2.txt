### Data Quality and Reliability Issues
While the dataset is largely functional, several issues affect the reliability of certain analyses:

*   **Missing Information:**
    *   **Customer Demographics:** Approximately 5.5% (23) of customer records are missing the `eta` (age) field, which limits the ability to perform complete age-based demographic segmentation [check_quality].

*   **Data Integrity and Consistency:**
    *   **Orphaned Records:** There are 11 rental records associated with `cliente_id`s that do not exist in the `clienti` table [check_quality]. This prevents these specific rentals from being attributed to a known customer profile, which could skew customer-centric reporting.
    *   **Duplicate Entries:** The `stazioni` table contains a duplicate entry for the "Piramide" station (`ST01`) [check_quality]. This could lead to double-counting station capacities or trip volumes if not handled carefully.
