### Data Overview
The dataset contains 1,464 total observations [tool]. The key variables are:
*   **Temperature**: The average maximum temperature was approximately **28.9°C** [tool], with a peak weekly mean of **35.9°C** occurring in late July [tool]. Minimum temperatures averaged **17.7°C** [tool].
*   **Rainfall**: The average daily rainfall was low, at approximately **1.6 mm**, though individual events reached up to **50.8 mm** [tool].
*   **Wind Speed**: The average maximum wind speed recorded was **24.9 km/h** [tool], with peaks up to **93 km/h** [tool].

### Data Quality and Reliability
The "solidity" of the data varies significantly depending on the variable:

1.  **Wind Speed (Not Solid)**: This is the most problematic variable. Approximately **26.5% of all wind measurements are missing** [tool]. Most critically, the stations **Tivoli, Cassino, and Terminillo have no wind data at all** (0% coverage) [tool]. Any attempt to describe wind patterns across the Lazio region would be biased and invalid because the data is missing systematically from entire locations.
2.  **Temperature and Rainfall (Mostly Solid)**: These variables are much more complete, though they are not perfect. There are missing values for both maximum temperature (~4.2%) and rainfall (~4.4%) [tool]. While this is relatively low, it means there are small gaps in the daily continuity for some stations.

### Limits and Conclusions
*   **Temperature**: The temperature trends are robust and show a clear summer progression, but because of the ~4% missingness, conclusions about specific daily extremes should be treated with caution.
*   **Wind**: **No conclusions can be drawn about wind speed** for the region. The data is heavily skewed towards a subset of stations (like Viterbo and Civitavecchia) and ignores others entirely.
*   **Next steps**: To make this analysis reliable, wind sensor data from the missing stations (Tivoli, Cassino, and Terminillo) must be recovered or the analysis must be strictly limited to the functional stations, clearly stating the geographical bias.
