    * **Session Duration**: 5.5% of sessions (80 total) are missing `durata_min` values [tool], which may bias engagement metrics if crashes or errors prevent duration from being recorded.
    * **Purchase Data**: 93.3% of the `acquisto_eur` entries are NULL [tool]. While it is expected that most sessions are not purchases, this means any revenue-based conclusions are based on a very small sample size of 98 sessions [tool].
* **Stability Concerns**: The 4.6% crash rate is high enough to warrant investigation, though the data does not currently show if these are concentrated on a specific device or platform [inference].

### Limits
* Conclusions are limited to the month of September 2026 and the specific user base captured in this export. 
* Because of the missing values, the average session duration and revenue metrics should be treated as estimates for the active paying/recorded user base rather than the entire population of users.
