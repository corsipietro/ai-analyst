### Data Quality Issues ("What is not solid")

*   **Missing Information:**
    *   **Customer Age:** 23 customers (approx. 5.5%) have no age recorded [tool].

*   **Relational Integrity:** There are 11 rental records associated with `cliente_id` values that do not exist in the `clienti` table [tool]. This means some revenue is being generated by "ghost" users who are not in your customer database.

### Limits
The conclusions drawn from this data are limited by the missing values and the presence of orphan records. For example, revenue analysis might be slightly skewed if the "orphan" customers follow different spending patterns than registered customers. I recommend cleaning the duplicates in the stations table and investigating why 11 rentals are not linked to valid customers before performing deep behavioral segmentation.
