*   **Broken Links (Orphaned Records):** There are 11 rental records that do not have a corresponding `cliente_id` in the customer table [tool]. This means the revenue and usage patterns for these 11 trips cannot be attributed to specific customer profiles (like age or membership type).

*   **Duplicate Records:** There is a duplicate entry for station `ST01` (Piramide) in the `stazioni` table [tool].

### Limits
