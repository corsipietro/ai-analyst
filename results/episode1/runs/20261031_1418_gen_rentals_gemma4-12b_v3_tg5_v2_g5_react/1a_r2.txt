*   **Revenue & Pricing**: The `importo_eur` column provides the basis for calculating revenue, which can be compared against the distance (`km`) or duration (`durata_min`) of the rides.
*   **Station Distribution**: The data shows where bikes are stationed and the capacity of those stations across various Roman neighborhoods (e.g., Ostiense, Trastevere, Garbatella).
