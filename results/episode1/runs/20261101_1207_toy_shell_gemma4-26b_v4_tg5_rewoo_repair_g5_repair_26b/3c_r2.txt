The behavior can be explained using the provided model equations for steady-state conditions.

1.  **Satellite Steady State:** In the long term, the number of satellites $S$ is determined by the balance of launches ($L$) and the natural retirement rate ($S/T$). Neglecting collisions for a moment, $L \approx S/T$, which means $S \approx L \cdot T$. With $T=5$, we expect $S \approx 5L$.

    *   The creation term is $f \cdot (\alpha \cdot S \cdot N)$.
    *   The decay term is $N/\tau$.
    *   Debris will grow if $f \cdot \alpha \cdot S \cdot N > N/\tau$. Dividing by $N$, the condition for growth is:
        $$f \cdot \alpha \cdot S > \frac{1}{\tau}$$
